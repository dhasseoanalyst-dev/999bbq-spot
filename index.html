About Us

Great BBQ Doesn't Need Four Walls

At 999BBQ Spot, we believe great food doesn't always need a brick-and-mortar address. We hit the streets with one vision: bring authentic, wood-smoked BBQ straight to you. Our mobile "BBQ taxi" is a unique fusion of flavor, mobility and convenience — a running kitchen that turns any street corner into a smokehouse.

  • High-quality meats, fresh produce & artisan spices — sourced with care
  • Slow-smoked over charcoal & wood for that deep, signature smoky aroma
  • Classic BBQ fused with local Coimbatore flavor — house marinades, sauces & sides
  • Built on a passion for food, hospitality & memorable experiences

Whether you're grabbing a bite on the go or booking us for your next event — we're here to fuel your BBQ cravings.
