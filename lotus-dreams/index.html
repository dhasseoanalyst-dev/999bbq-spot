Current status

As of 10 October 2026, the campaign is set up and submitted. It is in Meta's review or the first day of the learning phase.

  • Lead form with qualifying questions published

During the learning phase, results move up and down from day to day. That is normal. Editing the ads in this period restarts Meta's learning, so we will hold steady and judge results after the first week.

These are typical ranges for Coimbatore real estate on Meta, not a guarantee. Actual numbers depend on the creatives, the season and how quickly leads are followed up.

Not every lead is a buyer. As a rough guide, out of every 100 leads, expect about 30–40 genuine conversations and 5–10 site visits. The quality of follow-up decides how many of those become sales.

What improves these numbers:

  • Calling every lead within 15 minutes; after an hour, interest drops sharply
  • Sending photos, location and a walkthrough video on WhatsApp right after the call
  • Telling us which leads were good or junk, so we can tune the targeting

How leads reach you

Every form submission is saved in three places, so no lead is lost.

Privyr appInstantly, with a phone notificationCalling the lead within 15 minutes

Meta Leads CenterInstantlyBackup; downloadable for 90 days

Each row in the sheet shows the lead date, name, phone, and their answers on buying purpose, down payment, bank loan and location. Your team fills in the last three columns after calling:

  • Call Status: New, Called – no answer, Interested, Site visit booked, Not interested, or Junk
  • Called By: who made the call
  • Notes: anything useful, such as budget, preferred visit date or objections

We use these updates to see which ads bring real buyers, and adjust the campaign each week.
